A developer who starts with the problem.

EcoMart — Sustainable E-commerce Interface

An e-commerce interface that nudges shoppers toward greener choices through eco-friendly delivery defaults and visible carbon-impact information — designed, built and evaluated with real users.

Read the case study
Problem
Online shoppers rarely see the environmental cost of their choices at the moment they make them.
Solution
A shopping interface that makes sustainable options easy: eco-friendly delivery as the default and carbon-impact information alongside products.
My contribution
Interface design, front-end development, applying persuasive-technology principles and running the usability evaluation.
Key features
Product browsing, sustainable delivery defaults, carbon-impact indicators, responsive layout.
